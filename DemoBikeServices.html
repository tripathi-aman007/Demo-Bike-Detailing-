A few things to know before choosing a treatment for your motorcycle.

Ask about your bike ↗
Should I choose PPF or ceramic coating?

PPF is a physical film that can help protect covered paint from minor impacts and abrasion. Ceramic coating supports surface maintenance and gloss. The right choice depends on the surfaces, riding conditions and budget.

Can a wrap change my bike’s colour?

Vinyl can change the appearance of suitable panels. Coverage, panel condition and finish should be inspected first. Any required vehicle documentation should be checked before a colour change.

How much will my service cost?

Pricing depends on the motorcycle, surface condition, preparation required, product and coverage. Request an assessment for a clear quote.

How long will the treatment take?

A wash and a full protection installation have different time requirements. Preparation, paintwork and coating curing can affect turnaround. Confirm the schedule with the studio before booking.

Can polishing remove every scratch?

No. Some surface marks may improve with correction, while deeper damage can require paint repair. An inspection helps establish what can be safely achieved.
